Dr Ryan Shah

Researcher turned practitioner

I sit where academic rigour meets the unglamorous reality of getting an organisation certified, compliant and genuinely more resilient.

I hold a PhD in information and network security and privacy, and now work as Staff Engineer, Security Assurance & Audit at Nscale, after several years consulting in GRC and data protection.

My research, ten peer-reviewed papers on side-channels, IoT safety and traffic analysis, taught me to treat evidence as the product. In practice I turn frameworks into outcomes: vCISO, vISM and DPO engagements across health, finance and industry, and standards-based assurance explained to very different audiences, often under time pressure.

Career and education

  1. 2026 – now

    Staff Engineer, Security Assurance & Audit

    Nscale

    Hands-on lead for assurance and audit: SOC 2 Type II, ISO and customer commitments. I own audit readiness for new sites, one control framework across SOC 2, ISO and NIST, evidence standards, and the relationship with external auditors, working with GRC engineering on evidence automation.

  2. 2025 – 2026

    Data Protection Officer

    Sapphire, UK

    Independent oversight and advice on UK and EU data protection law, including the GDPR. I led privacy governance (policies, training, audits, records of processing), breach management and incident response, DPIAs and DSARs.

  3. 2024 – 2026

    Senior Security Consultant

    Sapphire, UK

    Led complex GRC engagements across compliance, assurance and risk. Product owner for Third-Party Risk Management, with vCISO and vISM support in healthcare, finance and industry. I led tenders across ISO 27001, 27701, 22301, 22237 and 42001, the GDPR, DORA, NIS 2, the NCSC CAF and NIST CSF.

  4. 2023 – 2024

    Security Consultant

    Sapphire, UK

    Subject-matter expert on DORA and NIS 2. I led ISO 27001, 22301 and 27701 engagements from gap analysis to internal audit and certification, plus awareness training and business continuity, across transport, logistics, education, healthcare, manufacturing and finance.

  5. 2022 – 2023

    Postdoctoral Researcher

    Heriot-Watt University, Edinburgh

    SECRIOUS project: how new-code and non-traditional entrants engage with cyber security in software engineering, with human-centred approaches to security education across cyber security, games research and HCI.

  6. 2018 – 2022

    PhD, Cyber Security

    University of Strathclyde

    Thesis: Security of Robotic Workflows. Calibration security and operational privacy in robotic, cyber-physical and safety-critical systems: passive side-channel threats, access control and blockchain-based approaches, using machine learning and signal processing.

  7. 2014 – 2018

    BSc Computer Science, First Class

    Heriot-Watt University

    Software development, networking and cyber security. My final-year project built a tool to identify and remediate IoT devices vulnerable to the Mirai malware in real time. Advanced coursework in network security, AI, machine learning and distributed systems.

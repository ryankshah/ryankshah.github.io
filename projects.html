PrivacyGraph

Evidence-backed map of how personal data may move through your code. Static analysis, local by default.

GoApache 2.0Static analysis
  • Links each finding to a file and line, names the detection method, assigns a confidence level and lists what remains unproven.
  • Reads Python, TypeScript, JavaScript and Go, plus Terraform, Docker Compose, Kubernetes and .env.example files.
  • Makes no network requests, sends no telemetry and calls no LLM while scanning.

Aether Bar

Replaces the Windows 11 taskbar with a floating bar you can put on any screen edge.

Windows 11No accountNo telemetry
Get Aether Bar
  • Five modes (Vibe, Util, Productive, Zen and Windows), plus modes you customise yourself.
  • Hover a running app to preview its windows, and drag an .exe onto the bar to pin it.
  • Floats or runs full width, and can hide until your cursor reaches the screen edge.
  • Follows your Windows theme and accent colour, and goes online only when you ask.
